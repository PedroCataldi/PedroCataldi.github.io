 We demonstrate the validity of the Background Substracion Technique using a mock galaxy catalogue, recovering the HOD from cosmoDC2 over the absolute magnitude range Mr = -20 to -17 and halo masses up to 1e15 Msun. We present key performance metrics, including completeness and purity, to quantify the precision and reliability of the group finder and the resulting HOD measurements.
